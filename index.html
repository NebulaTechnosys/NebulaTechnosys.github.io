FAQ

Common questions

I only have an idea — is that enough to start?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a rough sketch or even a verbal description. We help shape the concept into a design and then a prototype.

How much does prototyping cost?

It depends on size, complexity, material, and quantity. Share your project details and we'll provide a clear quote — usually within 24 hours.

What file formats do you accept?

STL, STEP, IGES, OBJ, and common CAD formats. Don't have files? No problem — we can design from your concept.

How long does a prototype take?

Simple prints can be ready in 1–3 days. Complex design + multi-iteration projects typically take 1–3 weeks. We'll give you a timeline upfront.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We treat every project confidentially and are happy to sign an NDA before discussing your idea.

Can you help with mass production?

We prepare production-ready designs and advise on manufacturing paths, tooling, and vendor selection for scaling beyond prototyping.
